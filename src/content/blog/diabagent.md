**DiabAgent** controls glucose in type 1 diabetes through medical-device tools. It
observes a sensor and its own records, acts through a pump, decides when to look
again, and is scored on a true glucose trajectory it never sees.

**SweetBench** evaluates it on 212 tasks built from 32 scenario templates. See the
[overview](/diabagent/), the [leaderboard](/diabagent/leaderboard/), the
[tasks](/diabagent/tasks/) and the [runs](/diabagent/runs/).
